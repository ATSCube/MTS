Who We Are

Your Trusted Automotive Partner Since 1992

The company was founded in 1992 for more than 30 years of building a relation based on trust and loyalty with our customers offering a full range of filters, oils, additives, batteries, lubricants for all type of passenger cars, industrial agricultural equipment.

At Mahdi Trading & Supply Co., we've built our reputation on three fundamental pillars: uncompromising quality, exceptional service, and deep industry expertise. Our journey began with a simple vision – to become Lebanon's most trusted automotive solutions provider.

Today, as the exclusive sole agents for industry-leading brands like Caldini Oils, Delsa and Kron Filters, we continue to uphold that vision by delivering products that exceed expectations and service that builds lasting partnerships.

Our Mission

To be the leading automotive supply partner in the region, delivering unparalleled quality and service excellence.
